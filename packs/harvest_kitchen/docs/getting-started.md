# Getting Started

Harvest Kitchen adds six new crops to Minecraft and a kitchen to cook them in. You grow the crops,
cut them, mix them and cook them into dishes. Every dish fills you up, and most of them also give
you a helpful effect.

When you join a world for the first time you get a [Recipe Poster](item:hk:recipe_poster). Hang
it on a wall: the first page shows the whole journey below. Sneak and use the poster to flip to
the recipes you have unlocked.

## Your first seeds

Break grass and you will sometimes find a new seed. Which seeds you find depends on where you
are: [tomatoes](crop:tomato) like [plains](biome:minecraft:plains), [chili peppers](crop:chili)
like the [jungle](biome:minecraft:jungle). Every seed can turn up anywhere, just less often. See
[Crops](page:crops) for the full list.

## Your first dish

[Tomato Soup](recipe:hk:tomato_soup) is the easiest dish to start with. You need two
[tomatoes](item:hk:tomato) and a [bowl](item:minecraft:bowl):

1. Craft them into a [Tomato Soup Mix](recipe:hk:tomato_soup_mix) on the crafting table.
2. Craft a [Cooking Pot](crafting:hk:cooking_pot) and put it on top of a
   [campfire](item:minecraft:campfire).
3. Use the pot with the mix in your hand, wait a few seconds, then use the pot again to take
   your soup out.

## Unlocking recipes

You don't have to remember any of this. Each time you find a new crop, the recipes that use it
unlock: they appear on your Recipe Poster and in the crafting table's recipe book. In Creative
mode everything is unlocked from the start.
