# Crafting

The kitchen tools are made on a normal crafting table. Each recipe appears in the recipe book as
soon as you pick up the thing that unlocks it, usually your first Simmerfield seed.

## Cooking Pot

The pot needs five iron ingots and a bowl. Its recipe also unlocks when you pick up iron or a
bowl.

```embed
crafting id=wm_kitchen:cooking_pot
```

## Cutting Board

A stick and two planks in a row, in any row of the crafting grid. Any kind of planks works.

```embed
crafting id=wm_kitchen:cutting_board
```

## Recipe Poster

Paper and any Simmerfield crop. It's small enough for the crafting grid in your inventory.
You also get one free the first time you join.

```embed
crafting id=wm_kitchen:recipe_poster
```

## Kitchen Guide

A book and a carrot, also small enough for your inventory's crafting grid. Use it to open the guide:
every recipe with its ingredients, the crops, and how to play. You get one free the first time you join.

```embed
crafting id=wm_kitchen:guide_book
```

## Kitchen decor

Eight blocks for building a kitchen. Each one turns to face you when you place it, and all but
the stove are only decoration.

- **Kitchen Counter** and **Kitchen Cabinet** come two at a time. Hang cabinets on the wall, at
  least two blocks above a sink so the tap fits.
- **Spice Shelf** and **Kitchen Sink** are decoration.
- **Kitchen Stove** heats a Cooking Pot on top of it while it's lit. Use the stove to switch it
  on or off; it glows and gives light while it's on. Sneak to put the pot on it.
- **Hanging Pan** goes on the underside of a block, like a ceiling or a cabinet.
- **Jar of Pickles** and **Vegetable Basket** stand on top of a block, such as a counter.
