# Simmerfield: marketing drafts

Everything in this folder is a **draft for the developer to edit**, written on 2026-10-22 from
`../plan.md` (v1 MUST scope), `../slice.md` and `../art-style.md`, before the portfolio slice was
tested in game. `marketplace.md` is the store text: a short and a long description, five feature
bullets, what not to claim, and the facts to confirm before publishing. `waypoint-pitch.md` is the
application to Waypoint Studios (what we make, why us, team, stack, timeline, the ask), with
placeholders in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` for personal details and an open statement of the AI-assisted
code and art pipeline. `portfolio-site.md` holds short blocks for your own site: a tagline, a
summary, a case study and technical highlights. `trailer.md` is the shot list for a ~48 s trailer
(with a 30 s cut) and setups for 8 screenshots, with flags on shots that depend on features not
yet seen working in game. "Simmerfield" is the final name (D1); store titles use "Simmerfield:
Farming & Cooking", credited "by Wendermods".
