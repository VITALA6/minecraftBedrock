# Stations

Three places do the work in Harvest Kitchen: the [Cutting Board](station:cutting_board), the
normal [crafting table](station:crafting_table) and the [Cooking Pot](station:cooking_pot).

```embed
stations
```

## Cutting Board

Hold a [tomato](item:hk:tomato), [onion](item:hk:onion), [cucumber](item:hk:cucumber) or
[eggplant](item:hk:eggplant) and use the board. You get slices or chopped pieces back, which go
into salads, sandwiches and skewers.

```embed
recipes station=cutting_board
```

## Cooking Pot

The pot cooks mixes into hot dishes. It only cooks with heat under it: put it on a campfire, fire,
lava or a magma block. It holds up to four mixes and cooks every mix of the same dish at once, so
four mixes give you four bowls in the time of one.

```embed
recipes station=cooking_pot
```

To make a pot, see [Crafting](page:crafting).
