# Crafting

The kitchen tools are made on a normal crafting table. Each recipe appears in the recipe book as
soon as you pick up the thing that unlocks it, usually your first Harvest Kitchen seed.

## Cooking Pot

The pot needs five iron ingots and a bowl. Its recipe also unlocks when you pick up iron or a
bowl.

```embed
crafting id=hk:cooking_pot
```

## Cutting Board

A stick and two planks in a row, in any row of the crafting grid. Any kind of planks works.

```embed
crafting id=hk:cutting_board
```

## Recipe Poster

Paper and any Harvest Kitchen crop. It's small enough for the crafting grid in your inventory.
You also get one free the first time you join.

```embed
crafting id=hk:recipe_poster
```
