## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-23

The first release of Simmerfield: grow new crops, cut them, mix them and cook them into dishes.

### Added

- **Six new crops.** Tomato, onion, cucumber and eggplant grow on farmland like wheat; chili
  peppers and strawberries grow on bushes that you pick and that grow new fruit. Find their
  seeds by breaking grass. Each seed is easier to find in the biome that crop likes, but every
  seed can turn up anywhere. The onion has no seed: plant an onion itself.
- **Cutting Board.** Use it with a tomato, onion, cucumber or eggplant to get slices or chopped
  pieces. Sneak to chop the whole stack. A knife chops on the board while you work.
- **Cooking Pot.** Put it over heat (a campfire, fire, lava, a magma block or a lit Kitchen
  Stove) and it cooks mixes into hot dishes. It holds up to four mixes and cooks every mix of the
  same dish at once. While it cooks, the liquid bubbles in the dish's colour and a spoon stirs;
  without heat it pauses and puts its lid on. Floating icons above the pot show what's still
  missing, and when more than one dish is possible it asks which one to cook.
- **Mixes.** Hot dishes take two steps: craft the raw ingredients into a mix on the crafting
  table, then cook the mix in the pot. There are 10 mixes, one per pot dish.
- **18 dishes**, most with a helpful effect such as Regeneration, Fire Resistance, Water
  Breathing, Night Vision or Speed:
  - Cooked in the pot: Tomato Soup, Vegetable Stew, Chili Stew, Fish Chowder, Chicken Soup,
    Onion Soup, Strawberry Jam, Stuffed Eggplant, Ratatouille and Eggplant Curry.
  - Made on the crafting table: Garden Salad, Salsa, Veggie Sandwich, Hearty Sandwich,
    Strawberry Cup, Eggplant Salad, Veggie Skewer, and a Strawberry Cake you place and eat a
    slice at a time.
  - Bowl dishes give the bowl back, and the skewer gives its stick back.
- **Recipe Poster.** A two-block wall poster. Its first page shows how to get started; sneak and
  use it to flip through the recipes you have unlocked. Crafted from paper and any Simmerfield
  crop.
- **Kitchen Guide.** A book with every recipe and its steps, the crops, and how to play. Crafted
  from a book and a carrot.
- **Recipe unlocks.** Each crop you find unlocks the recipes that use it, on your poster, in the
  guide and in the crafting table's recipe book. In Creative mode everything is unlocked from the
  start.
- **A welcome on your first join**, with a free Kitchen Guide and Recipe Poster.
- **Eight kitchen decor blocks:** Kitchen Counter, Kitchen Cabinet, Spice Shelf, Kitchen Sink,
  Kitchen Stove, Hanging Pan, Jar of Pickles and Vegetable Basket. Each faces you when placed.
  The Kitchen Stove switches on and off when you use it, glows while it's lit, and heats a
  Cooking Pot on top of it.
- **Eight languages:** English, Ukrainian, Polish, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French
  and Russian.
- **`/wm_kitchen:debug` for operators**, to help check a world: a kit of every item, instant crop
  growth, fast cooking, and the state of a pot.
