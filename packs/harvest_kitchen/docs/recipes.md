# Recipes

Hot dishes take two steps: craft the raw ingredients into a mix, then cook the mix in the
[Cooking Pot](station:cooking_pot). Cold dishes are crafted straight away. Bowl dishes give you
the bowl back when you eat them.

## Effect colours

Most dishes give an effect for a while after you eat them. The colour tells you what kind of
effect it is.
