# Crops

There are six crops. You find their seeds by breaking grass, and each seed is easier to find in
the places that crop likes. Every seed can still turn up anywhere, so you're never stuck.

```embed
crops
```

## Growing

Most crops are planted on farmland, like wheat. Break them when they're fully grown to get the
crop and some seeds back. The [onion](crop:onion) has no seeds: plant an
[onion](item:hk:onion) itself.

[Chili peppers](crop:chili) and [strawberries](crop:strawberry) grow on bushes, like sweet
berries. Plant them on grass or dirt, and when they're ripe, use the bush to pick the fruit. The
bush stays and grows new fruit.

## What to do with them

Raw crops go into mixes and cold dishes. Some recipes need them cut first: see
[Stations](page:stations). Once you've found a crop, its recipes unlock on your
[Recipe Poster](item:hk:recipe_poster).
